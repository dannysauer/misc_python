Prediction error with and without a term for stored heat, captured 2026-11-15.

Source: a throwaway prototype written while the thermal model was being
designed. It is not in the repository, so these numbers cannot be reproduced
from it. The repository checks only part of the claim:
tests/test_training.py::test_train_predicts_a_house_whose_walls_store_heat
shows that the model with the term beats "no change" fourfold. Nothing in the
repository reproduces the rows without the term.

What it ran: 60 days of a simulated house whose air trades heat with walls ten
times heavier than itself, read through an exact sensor and through one with
0.1 °C of noise rounded to 0.5 °C. Each model was fitted to the oldest four
fifths and replayed over the newest fifth, starting a run every hour from the
measured temperature. The columns are the mean absolute error, in °C, 1, 2, 4
and 8 hours ahead. "No change" assumes the temperature stays where it started.

The walls of the simulated house lag by 1 / (0.05 + 0.02) = 14.3 hours.
